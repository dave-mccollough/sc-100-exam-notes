# Role Based Access Control

- [Link][(](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/role-based-access-control/overview))

- Role based access control (RBAC)
  - A security model that controls access to resources based on assigned roles and permissions
  - Ensures users have only the access necessary to perform their responsibilities
  - Follows the principles of least privilege

- Principles of least privilege
  - Give out the least amount of rights for the user to their job
  - Mixed with PIM, you can achieve just in time (JIT) administration
  - Encourged by Microsoft
