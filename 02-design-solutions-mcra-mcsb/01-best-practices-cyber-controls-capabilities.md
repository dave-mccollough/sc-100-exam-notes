# Design solutions that align with best practices for cybersecurity capabilities and controls

- Zero Trust Model
  - [Link](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/security/zero-trust/)
  - Always assume breach
  - Verify explicity
  - Always authenticate
  - Give authorization based on who the user or device is
  - Operate on the principals of least privilge model
  - Just in time(JIT) administration
  - Just enough access(JEA) administration
  - Privileged Identity Management
    - [Link](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/id-governance/privileged-identity-management/pim-configure)
    - Schedule access 
    - Conditional Access Policies
      - Geographic, Time, etc 
    - Compliance Policies
      - Controls whether a device can connect based on device criteria, OS, OS Version, updates etc
