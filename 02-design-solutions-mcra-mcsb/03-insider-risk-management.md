# Insider Risk Management

- Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management
  - Detect, investigate and act on malicious or inadverent activities in your organization
  - Insider risk policies allow you to define the types of risks to identify and detect
  - Act on or escalate cases to eDiscovery (Premium)

- Modern Internal Risks
  - Leaks or spillage of sensitive data
  - Confidentiality violations
  - IP theft
  - Fraud
  - Insider Trading
  - Regulatory compliance violations

- Principles of Insider Risk Management
  - Transparency
    - Balance user privacy vs org risk
    - Privacy by design architecture
  - Configurable
    - Policies configured based on industry, location/geography or business requirements
  - Integrated
    - Integrated workflow across Purview solutions
  - Actionable
    - Insigts to enable reviewer notifications, data intestigations and user investigations

- Insider Risk Management Workflow
  - Policy
    - Pre defined templates and policy conditions
      - Includes
        - Data theft by departing users
        - Data leaks
        - Data leaks by priority users
        - Data leaks by risky users
        - Security policy violations
        - Security policy violations by departing users
        - Security polciy violations by risky users
        - Security policy violations by priority users
        - Patient data misuse
        - Risky browser usage
  - Alerts
    - Alerts Dashboard
      - Automatically generated by risk indicators
        - Includes
          - ID
          - Users 
          - Alert
          - Status
          - Alert Severity
          - Time Detected
          - Case 
          - Case Status
          - Risk Factors

  - Triage
  - Investigate
  - Action
    - eDiscovery
    - Office 365 Management APIs
