# SIEM and SOAR

- Microsoft Sentinel is a SIEM and SOAR product
- Collections and aggregates data from a wide variety of sources
- Uses analytics and AI to identify threats 
- Provides tools for detailed investigations of alerts and automates response actions
- Offers connectors for services inside the outside the Microsoft ecosystem

- Security Information and Event Management(SIEM)
  - Collects data - data ingestion
    - Users
    - Apps
    - Devices
    - Infrastructure
      - On prem and cloud
  - Performs data normalization after ingestion
  - Uses AI and threat intelligence to detect, alert and investigate threats and anomolies

- Security Orchestration, Automation and Response(SOAR)
  - Helps teams respond to incidents
  - Uses playbooks to automate responses to common threats
  - Integrates various tools and services to reduce time and effort for incident response and management
