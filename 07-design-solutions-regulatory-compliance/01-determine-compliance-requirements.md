# Determine Compliance Requirements

- [Link](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/purview-compliance)

- Determine Compliance Requirements leveraging Microsoft Purview
  - Communications Compliance
    - Monitor org communications identifying and mitigating risks related to
      - Sensitive information
      - Inappropiate content
  - Data lifecycle management
    - Retention and deletion of org data across multiple platformss
    - Align regulatory requirements 
    - Reduce data exposure risks
  - Records management
    - Manage record keeping obligations supporting regulatory and legal requirements
    - Optimize data retention and deletion processes
  - Audit solutions
    - Capture user and admin activities across M365 services
  - eDiscovery
    - Identification, collection and review of electronic information for legal and regulatory purposes
  - Compliance Manager
    - Workflow management
    - Control implementation
    - Evidence cataloging
  - Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
    - Helps prevent unauthorized sharing, use or transfer of sensitive data
    - Uses a centralized policy mannagement with information protection
