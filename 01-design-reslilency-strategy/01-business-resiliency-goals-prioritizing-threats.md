# Business resiliency goals and prioritizing threats

## Business Resiliency Goals

- High Availability
  - Ensuring services are available without interruption
  - Involves deploying resources across multiple Azure regions or availability zones
  - Provides redundancy and failover capabilities

- Disaster Recovery
  - Preparing for incidents that may cause downtime or data loss
    - Natural disasters, cyberattacks, etc
  - Involves data backup, replication and recovery processes

- Data Durability
  - Ensuring data is not lost or corrupted over time
  - Geo-redundant storage
    - Replicates data across geographically dispersed data centers
  
  - Operational responsiveness
    - Ability to detect, diagnose and address issues
    - Azure tools
      - Azure monitor
      - Azure service health

- Compliance and security
  - Maintaining compliance for regulatory standards
  - Ensure security of data and applications

## Identifying and Prioritizing Threats

- Risk Assessment
  - Identify potential threats to business operations
  - Analyzing internal and external factors
    - Software vulnerabilities
    - Hardware failures
    - Network disruptions
    - Human factors
    - Natural Disasters

- Threat Modeling
  - Identify potential security threats and vulnerabilities and determine their impact to business operations
  - Understand attack surface and prioritize security measures

- Criticality Analysis
  - Identifying and prioritizing resources based on importance to the business
  - Critical services have largest impact to business

- Compliance Requirements
  - Compliance requirements can guide the identification and prioritization of threats

- Continuous Monitoring and Analysis
  - Continuous monitoring of security posture and operational health
  - Helps identify emerging threats and vulnerabilities
