# Azure Landing Zones

- [Link](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ready/landing-zone/?tabs=conceptual%2Chubspoke)

- Framework or strategy for implementing key design principles
- Uses subscriptions to scale application and platform resources
- Management groups contain different subscripotions

- Pillars
  - Identity and access management

- Platform Based Landing Zone
  - Subscripotion that provides shared services to applications in application landing zones
    - Identity 
    - Connectivity
    - Management

- Application Based Landing Zone
  - Subscription for hosting your application
