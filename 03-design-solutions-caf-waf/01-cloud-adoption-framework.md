  - Manage

    - Operations maturity
  - Govern
    - Business risks
    - Policy and compliance

- Key Points
  - Strategic Planning 
    - Align cloud adoption with with business goals and objectives
  - Governance and Compliance
    - Governance and compliance mechanisms to manage risks, ensure data security comply with requlatory requirements
    - Establish clear policies and practices for cloud operations
  - Cloud Adoption Methodologies
    - Methodologies for adopting cloud technologies
    - Covers initial migrations to cloud native development
  - Skills Readiness and Learning Paths
    - Recognizes the importance of having a skilled workforce
  - Tools and Resources
    - Tools, templates and resources for various stages of cloud adoption
      - Inital planning to deployment and optimization
  - Best Practices and Benchmarks
    - Insights and recommendations for a successful cloud adoption
  - Continuous Optimization and Management
    - Focused on helping orgs optimize and manage their cloud environment
