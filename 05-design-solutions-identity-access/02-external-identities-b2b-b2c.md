# External Identities - B2B and B2C

- [Link](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/external-id/external-identities-overview)

- Entra Admin Center -> External Identities -> Cross Tenant Access Settings

- Securely interact with users outside your organization

- B2B
  - Business to Business
    - B2B Collaboration
      - Allows external users to use their preferred identity to sign in to your Microsoft applications or other enterprise apps
      - Appear in directory as Guest Users
    - B2B Direct
      - Establishes a two way trust 
      - Seamless collaboration
      - Supports Teams shared channels
      - Users aren't represented in the directory, but are visible in the Teams shared channels and can be monitored in Teams admin center reports

- B2C
  - Business to Customer/Consumer
    - Entra B2C
      - Use Entra B2C for identity and access management on custom apps
      - Uses a seperate directory than your main directory
