# Multi Factor Authentication - MFA

- [Link](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/authentication/concept-mfa-howitworks)

- A security mechanism that requires users to verify their identity using two or more authentication factors, such as something they know (password), something they have (security key or phone), or something they are (biometrics), to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

- Something you know
  - Password, Pin, etc
- Something you have
  - Cell phone, fob, keycard
- Something you are
  - Biometrics

- Secure identities with two step verification
